Method and limitations

EPANET emitter scenarios are matched against sparse pressure and pipe-flow readings while varying overall consumer demand. Potential recovery = fitted emitter outflow × 86.4 m³/day per L/s × recovery fraction.

*Relative fit weights are a Gaussian-like softmax of hydraulic mismatch scores. They are not calibrated probabilities. The triage index is a heuristic (weight × possible water recovery), not expected actual savings, repair cost, or economic return.

Assumes one leak at a junction in a demand-driven, steady-state model. No uncertainty for pipe roughness, unmodelled demands, multiple leaks, repairs, or temporal pressure variation. Candidate sites are not confirmed leaks. Not suitable for directing real-world repair work without verification.
